carbon_pipeline
===============

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of carbon_pipeline is based on the CO2 transport data of the carbon capture, transport and storage catalogue of the Danish Energy Agency.

**Citation**

Danish Energy Agency (2026). Technology Data for Carbon Capture, Transport and Storage. Danish Energy Agency. https://ens.dk/en/analyses-and-statistics/technology-data-carbon-capture-transport-and-storage

capacity_addition_unbounded
---------------------------

Step 1
The unbounded capacity addition is the size of the Duiven carbon capture plant (0.1 MtCO2 per year).

max_diffusion_rate
------------------

Step 1
The maximum diffusion rate of carbon_pipeline is set to the historically observed diffusion rate of wind onshore from Mannhardt et al. (2024).

**Citation**

Jacob Mannhardt, Paolo Gabrielli, Giovanni Sansavini (2024). Understanding the vicious cycle of myopic foresight and constrained technology deployment in transforming the European energy system. iScience. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.111369

capex_per_distance_transport
----------------------------

Step 1
The distance-specific investment cost of carbon_pipeline is based on Technology Data for Carbon Capture, Transport and Storage. On the 36 edges that do not connect two adjacent countries, the offshore cost of 3.969e+04 Euro/(tCO2/h)/km applies.

**Citation**

Danish Energy Agency (2026). Technology Data for Carbon Capture, Transport and Storage. Danish Energy Agency. https://ens.dk/en/analyses-and-statistics/technology-data-carbon-capture-transport-and-storage
