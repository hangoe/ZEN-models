Step 1
Steel demand data is derived from multiple sources. The main sourceis the Aidres dataset. Additional manual data for missing countries is obtained from the following datasets:Eurofer, WorldSteel, and TradeEconomics. The secondary steel ratios are derived from the Material Economics dataset.

**Citations**

- **aidres**: Luc Girardin, Juan Correa Laguna, Joris Valee (2023). Advancing industrial decarbonisation by assessing the future use of renewable energies in industrial processes. Publications Office of the European Union. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/d80943a6-5116-11ee-9220-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
- **eurofer**: Eurofer (2025). European Steel in Figures 2025. Eurofer. https://www.eurofer.eu/publications/brochures-booklets-and-factsheets/european-steel-in-figures-2025
- **worldsteel**: World Steel (2023). 2023 World Steel in Figures. World Steel. https://worldsteel.org/data/world-steel-in-figures/world-steel-in-figures-2023/
- **trade_economics**: Trade Economics (2020). Norway Steel Production. Trade Economics. https://tradingeconomics.com/norway/steel-production
- **material_economics**: Material Economics (2019). Industrial Transformation 2050 - Pathways to Net-Zero Emissions from EU Heavy Industry. Material Economics. https://materialeconomics.com/publications/publication/industrial-transformation-2050
