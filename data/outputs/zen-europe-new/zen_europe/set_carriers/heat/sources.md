demand
------

Step 1
The heat demand data is derived from multiple sources:First, the JRC IDEES dataset provides useful thermal energy services for most countries. Second, the Eurostat dataset offers residential heat demand data for NO and UK. Third, the DESNZ dataset contains tertiary heat demand for the UK. Fourth, the BFE dataset provides heat demand data for CH. Finally, the Energifaktanorge dataset provides tertiary heat demand for NO.
