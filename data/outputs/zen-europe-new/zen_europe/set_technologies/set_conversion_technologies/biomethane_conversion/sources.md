biomethane_conversion
=====================

lifetime
--------

Step 1
Technical lifetime for 'biomethane_conversion' is the median across all available data for the agencies dea reporting data for this technology at plant size 'None'. Monetary values are rebased to 2025 Euro using ECB HICP inflation.

capacity_existing
-----------------

Step 1
The existing capacity of biomethane conversion is obtained from the European Biogas Association's biomethane production statistics. Capacities up to 2020 are per-plant values from the 2020 biomethane map. The 2026 map reports plant counts only, so additions after the 2020 map are the difference between the two editions' counts, allocated to years with the European yearly profile printed on the 2026 map and valued at an average capacity per plant calibrated to the 8.2 bcm/yr EBA reports for 2026.

**Citation**

European Biogas Association (EBA) (2026). EBA-GiE biomethane map 2020 and 2026. EBA-GiE biomethane map. https://www.europeanbiogas.eu/publication/european-biomethane-map-2026/. Note: Per-plant capacities and commissioning years are from the 2020 edition (https://www.europeanbiogas.eu/publication/eba-gie-biomethane-map-2020/). The 2026 edition reports plant counts only and is entered as constants on the dataset class.

max_diffusion_rate
------------------

Step 1
The maximum diffusion rate of biomethane_conversion is set to the historically observed diffusion rate of wind onshore from Mannhardt et al. (2024).

**Citation**

Jacob Mannhardt, Paolo Gabrielli, Giovanni Sansavini (2024). Understanding the vicious cycle of myopic foresight and constrained technology deployment in transforming the European energy system. iScience. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.111369
