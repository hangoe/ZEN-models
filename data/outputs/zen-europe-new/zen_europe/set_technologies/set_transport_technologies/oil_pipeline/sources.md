oil_pipeline
============

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of oil pipelines is manually set to 60 years, in line with the lifetime of the other linear transport infrastructure.

transport_loss_factor_linear
----------------------------

Step 1
The transport losses of oil pipelines are manually set to 5e-5 per km, in line with the losses of the other linear transport infrastructure.

capex_per_distance_transport
----------------------------

Step 1
The investment cost of oil_pipeline is based on the gas pipeline investment cost of the TYNDP 2022 scenario building guidelines (Tab. 14, p. 32).

**Citation**

ENTSOG, ENTSO-E (2021). TYNDP 2022 Scenario Building Guidelines. ENTSOG and ENTSO-E. https://www.entsog.eu/sites/default/files/2021-10/entsos_TYNDP_2022_Scenario_Building_Guidelines_211007_1.pdf
