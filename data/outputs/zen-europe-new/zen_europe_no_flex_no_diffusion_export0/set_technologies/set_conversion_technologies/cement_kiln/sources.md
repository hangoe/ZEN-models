conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The conversion factor of cement kilns is derived from a fuel consumption of 3.7 GJ per ton of clinker  (Material Economics) and an electricity consumption of 0.29 GJ per ton of cement (AIDRES), rescaled to a clinker basis via the AIDRES clinker-to-cement ratio of 0.70. The reference cement fuel is hard coal, which can be substituted by other fuels in the model ('<x>_to_cement_fuel').
