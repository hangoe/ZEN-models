capacity_existing
-----------------

Step 1
The existing production capacity is the JRC-IDEES installed capacity (glass, paper) at 8760 hours per year; for ceramic, food and the paper nodes without JRC-IDEES data it is the product demand (see demand) divided by an assumed capacity utilisation of 0.8 (general industry assumptions). It is assumed to be built evenly over the lifetime of the technology.

conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The energy intensity is the activity-weighted energy intensity of the sector's sub-processes from Rehfeldt et al. (2017) (glass: AIDRES (2023) routes, weighted by EU production share). Heat below 200 °C is split into temperature bands with the Rehfeldt temperature distribution and, for 100-200 °C, with Wolf (2017), and converted from final energy to useful heat with a reference boiler efficiency of 90%; the rest is fuel, split by the JRC-IDEES fuel mix. The natural gas of glass and ceramic is supplied as kiln fuel; for ceramic, an assumed 97.4 % of it can switch fuel, since the ceramic BREF (JRC, Final Draft, April 2026, Sections 4.2.1-4.2.3) limits electrified kilns to below 1600 °C and the > 1000 °C share of technical ceramics in Rehfeldt et al. (2017) is treated as not switchable.
