salt_cavern_storage
===================

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of salt_cavern_storage is based on the hydrogen storage caverns of the energy storage catalogue of the Danish Energy Agency. The caverns are assumed to exist already, so they do not retire within the modeling horizon.

**Citation**

Danish Energy Agency (2026). Technology Data for Energy Storage. Danish Energy Agency. https://ens.dk/en/analyses-and-statistics/technology-data-energy-storage. Note: Hydrogen storage caverns, assuming pre-existing salt caverns.

opex_specific_fixed
-------------------

Step 1
Fixed operational cost for 'salt_cavern_storage' is the mean across all available data for the agencies victoria reporting data for this technology at plant size 'None'. Monetary values are rebased to 2025 Euro using ECB HICP inflation.

efficiency_charge
-----------------

Step 1
The round-trip efficiency of salt_cavern_storage is 0.99 in the energy storage catalogue of the Danish Energy Agency and is split evenly between charging and discharging.

**Citation**

Danish Energy Agency (2026). Technology Data for Energy Storage. Danish Energy Agency. https://ens.dk/en/analyses-and-statistics/technology-data-energy-storage. Note: Hydrogen storage caverns, assuming pre-existing salt caverns.
