Step 1
Ammonia demand from Gabrielli et al. (2023) and the International Fertilizer Association (IFA). The data was first produced by IFA and then processed by Gabrielli et al. (2023). Converted from 1000 tNH3 to GW (assume constant demand) and interpolated to fill missing years; the yearly variation is expressed relative to the reference year.

**Citation**

Paolo Gabrielli, Lorenzo Rosa, Matteo Gazzani, Raoul Meys, André Bardow, Marco Mazzotti, Giovanni Sansavini, International Fertilizer Association (2023). Ammonia demand from Gabrielli et al. (2023) and the International Fertilizer Association (IFA). One Earth. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590332223002075
