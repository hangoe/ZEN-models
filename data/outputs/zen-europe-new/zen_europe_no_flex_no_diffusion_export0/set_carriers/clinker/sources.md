demand
------

Step 1
Clinker demand data is derived from the Aidres dataset, which provides demand data for various industrial sectors. For countries not covered in the Aidres dataset,  cement demand data from the British Geological Survey is used.

**Citations**

Publications Office of the European Union. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/d80943a6-5116-11ee-9220-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
