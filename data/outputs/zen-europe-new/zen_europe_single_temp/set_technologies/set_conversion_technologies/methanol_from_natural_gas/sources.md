methanol_from_natural_gas
=========================

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime for methanol from natural gas technologies is based on the work of Collodi et al. (2017).

**Citation**

Guido Collodi, Giuliana Azzaro, Noemi Ferrari, Stanley Santos (2017). Demonstrating Large Scale Industrial CCS through CCU - A Case Study for Methanol Production. Energy Procedia. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2021.115052

capacity_existing
-----------------

Step 1
The existing capacity for methanol from natural gas is derived from the methanol demand data. We assume that all current methanol plants use natural gas as feedstock. 

**Citations**

- **aidres**: Luc Girardin, Juan Correa Laguna, Joris Valee (2023). Advancing industrial decarbonisation by assessing the future use of renewable energies in industrial processes. Publications Office of the European Union. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/d80943a6-5116-11ee-9220-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
- **wits**: World Bank (2022). Methanol Imports and Exports by Country. World Bank. https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/CHE/year/2022/tradeflow/Imports/partner/ALL/product/290511
- **equinor**: Equinor (2026). Tjeldbergodden industrial facility. Equinor. https://www.equinor.com/energy/tjeldbergodden
- **chem_analyst**: World Bank (2023). UK Methanol Market Analysis. World Bank. https://www.chemanalyst.com/industry-report/united-kingdom-methanol-market-206

max_diffusion_rate
------------------

Step 1
The maximum diffusion rate of methanol_from_natural_gas is set to the historically observed diffusion rate of wind onshore from Mannhardt et al. (2024).

**Citation**

Jacob Mannhardt, Paolo Gabrielli, Giovanni Sansavini (2024). Understanding the vicious cycle of myopic foresight and constrained technology deployment in transforming the European energy system. iScience. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.111369
