H2_DRI
======

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of steel production technologies is derived as the average of the financial and technical lifetimes assumed in Agora Industry (2021), 'Low-carbon technologies for the global steel transformation'.

**Citation**

Wido K. WiteckaJulian SomersKathy ReimannNiklas WagnerOle ZeltAlexander JülichClemens SchneiderProf. Max Åhman (2024). Low-carbon technologies for the global steel transformation. Agora Industry. https://www.agora-industry.org/publications/low-carbon-technologies-for-the-global-steel-transformation

-------------------------

Step 1

Martin Woertler, Felix Schuler, Nicole Voigt, Torben Schmidt, Peter Dahlmann, Hans Bodo Lüngen, Jean-Theo Ghenda (2013). Steel's contribution to a low-carbon Europe 2050: Technical and economic analysis of the sector's CO2 abatement potential. Boston Consulting Group. https://www.stahl-online.de//wp-content/uploads/Schlussbericht-Studie-Low-carbon-Europe-2050_-Mai-20131.pdf
