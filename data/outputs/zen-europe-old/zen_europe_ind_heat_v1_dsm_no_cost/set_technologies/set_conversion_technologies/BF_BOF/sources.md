BF_BOF
======

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of steel production technologies is derived as the average of the financial and technical lifetimes assumed in Agora Industry (2021), 'Low-carbon technologies for the global steel transformation'.

**Citation**

Wido K. WiteckaJulian SomersKathy ReimannNiklas WagnerOle ZeltAlexander JülichClemens SchneiderProf. Max Åhman (2024). Low-carbon technologies for the global steel transformation. Agora Industry. https://www.agora-industry.org/publications/low-carbon-technologies-for-the-global-steel-transformation

capacity_existing
-----------------

Step 1
It is assumed that all current primary steel demand is based on the BF-BOF route, and all current secondary steel demand is basedon the EAF route. Steel demand data is derived from multiple sources. The main sourceis the Aidres dataset. Additional manual data for missing countries is obtained from the following datasets:Eurofer, WorldSteel, and TradeEconomics. The secondary steel ratios are derived from the Material Economics dataset.

**Citations**

- **aidres**: Luc Girardin, Juan Correa Laguna, Joris Valee (2023). Advancing industrial decarbonisation by assessing the future use of renewable energies in industrial processes. Publications Office of the European Union. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/d80943a6-5116-11ee-9220-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
- **eurofer**: Eurofer (2025). European Steel in Figures 2025. Eurofer. https://www.eurofer.eu/publications/brochures-booklets-and-factsheets/european-steel-in-figures-2025
- **worldsteel**: World Steel (2023). 2023 World Steel in Figures. World Steel. https://worldsteel.org/data/world-steel-in-figures/world-steel-in-figures-2023/
- **trade_economics**: Trade Economics (2020). Norway Steel Production. Trade Economics. https://tradingeconomics.com/norway/steel-production
- **material_economics**: Material Economics (2019). Industrial Transformation 2050 - Pathways to Net-Zero Emissions from EU Heavy Industry. Material Economics. https://materialeconomics.com/publications/publication/industrial-transformation-2050

max_diffusion_rate
------------------

Step 1
The maximum diffusion rate of BF_BOF is set to the historically observed diffusion rate of wind onshore from Mannhardt et al. (2024).

**Citation**

Jacob Mannhardt, Paolo Gabrielli, Giovanni Sansavini (2024). Understanding the vicious cycle of myopic foresight and constrained technology deployment in transforming the European energy system. iScience. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.111369
