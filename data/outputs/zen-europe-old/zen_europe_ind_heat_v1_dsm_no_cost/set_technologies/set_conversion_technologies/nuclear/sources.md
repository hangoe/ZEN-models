nuclear
=======

lifetime
--------

Step 1
Technical lifetime for 'nuclear' is the median across all available data for the agencies diw, euref, luw, potencia reporting data for this technology at plant size 'None'. Monetary values are rebased to 2022 Euro using ECB HICP inflation.

**Citations**

- **dea**: Danish Energy Agency (2026). Technology catalogues. Danish Energy Agency. https://ens.dk/en/analyses-and-statistics/technology-catalogues. Note: Includes data for electricity, district heating, individual heating,renewable fuels, carbon capture, and more technologies.

ENTSOG/ENTSO-E. https://2020.entsos-tyndp-scenarios.eu/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/TYNDP_2020_Scenario_Building-Guidelines_03_Annex_2_Cost_Assumptions_final_report.pdf. Note: Cost figures transcribed into TYNDP_cost_assumptions.xlsx from the PDF above.

capacity_existing
-----------------

Step 1
The existing capacity data is derived from the PowerPlantMatching dataset.

**Citation**

Fabian Gotzens, Heidi Heinrichs, Jonas Hörsch, Fabian Hofmann (2019). Performing energy modelling exercises in a transparent way - The issue of data quality in power plant databases. Energy Strategy Reviews. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esr.2018.11.004

capacity_limit
--------------

Step 1
The capacity limit is set to 0, as the nuclear phase-out is considered.

max_load
--------

Step 1
The maximum load (technical availability) data is derived from the Potencia dataset.

**Citation**

European Commission Joint Research Centre (2018). POTEnCIA Central scenario 2018 - technology cost and performance projections. European Commission, JRC POTEnCIA model. https://ec.europa.eu/jrc/potencia

----

Step 2
The seasonal maximum load for nuclear is derived from the ENTSO-E generation and capacity data.

**Citations**

- **entsoe**: ENTSO-E (2026). ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. https://transparency.entsoe.eu/. Note: Queried via the entsoe-py package against the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform RESTful API; requires an API security token in the ENTSOE_API_KEY environment variable.

max_diffusion_rate
------------------

Step 1
The maximum diffusion rate of nuclear is set to the historically observed diffusion rate of wind offshore from Mannhardt et al. (2024).

**Citation**

Jacob Mannhardt, Paolo Gabrielli, Giovanni Sansavini (2024). Understanding the vicious cycle of myopic foresight and constrained technology deployment in transforming the European energy system. iScience. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.111369

conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The conversion factor is derived from the Eurostat dataset as the total consumption of uranium divided by the total electricity generation from nuclear.

**Citation**

Eurostat (2026). Eurostat energy balances. Eurostat. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_bal_c/default/table?lang=en
