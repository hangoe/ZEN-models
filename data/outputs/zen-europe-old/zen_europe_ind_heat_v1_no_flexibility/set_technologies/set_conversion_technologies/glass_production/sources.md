glass_production
================

lifetime
--------

Step 1
JRC-EU-TIMES costs and lifetime of the glass processes, weighted by the AIDRES (2023) EU production shares of the glass routes. Costs in 2022 Euro using ECB HICP inflation, see docs, 'Production technologies'.

conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The energy intensity is the activity-weighted energy intensity of the sector's sub-processes from Rehfeldt et al. (2017) (glass: AIDRES (2023) routes, weighted by EU production share). Heat below 200 °C is split into temperature bands with the Rehfeldt temperature distribution and, for 100-200 °C, with Wolf (2017); the rest is fuel, split by the JRC-IDEES fuel mix.
