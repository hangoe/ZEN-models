hydrogen_FC_ship
================

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of hydrogen_FC_ship is based on Korberg et al. (2021).

**Citation**

A.D. Korberg, S. Brynolf, M. Grahn, I.R. Skov (2021). Techno-economic assessment of advanced fuels and propulsion systems in future fossil-free ships. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.110861

max_load
--------

Step 1
The maximum load of hydrogen_FC_ship is based on Korberg et al. (2021). Ships are assumed to operate at 75% of nominal capacity for 5280 hours per year.

**Citation**

A.D. Korberg, S. Brynolf, M. Grahn, I.R. Skov (2021). Techno-economic assessment of advanced fuels and propulsion systems in future fossil-free ships. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.110861

opex_specific_variable
----------------------

Step 1
The variable opex of hydrogen FC ships is manually derived from the fuel distribution cost, based on Korberg et al. (2021).

**Citation**

A.D. Korberg, S. Brynolf, M. Grahn, I.R. Skov (2021). Techno-economic assessment of advanced fuels and propulsion systems in future fossil-free ships. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.110861

opex_specific_fixed
-------------------

Step 1
The specific fixed opex of hydrogen FC ships is obtained from Korberg et al. (2021).

**Citation**

A.D. Korberg, S. Brynolf, M. Grahn, I.R. Skov (2021). Techno-economic assessment of advanced fuels and propulsion systems in future fossil-free ships. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.110861

max_diffusion_rate
------------------

Step 1
The maximum diffusion rate of hydrogen_FC_ship is set to the historically observed diffusion rate of wind onshore from Mannhardt et al. (2024).

**Citation**

Jacob Mannhardt, Paolo Gabrielli, Giovanni Sansavini (2024). Understanding the vicious cycle of myopic foresight and constrained technology deployment in transforming the European energy system. iScience. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.111369

capex_specific_conversion
-------------------------

Step 1
The specific capex of hydrogen FC ships is obtained from Korberg et al. (2021). The selected technology is: Fuel cell hydrogen.

**Citation**

A.D. Korberg, S. Brynolf, M. Grahn, I.R. Skov (2021). Techno-economic assessment of advanced fuels and propulsion systems in future fossil-free ships. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.110861

conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The conversion factor of hydrogen FC ships is obtained from Korberg et al. (2021). The electricity input accounts for the liquefaction energy demand of hydrogen.

**Citation**

A.D. Korberg, S. Brynolf, M. Grahn, I.R. Skov (2021). Techno-economic assessment of advanced fuels and propulsion systems in future fossil-free ships. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.110861
