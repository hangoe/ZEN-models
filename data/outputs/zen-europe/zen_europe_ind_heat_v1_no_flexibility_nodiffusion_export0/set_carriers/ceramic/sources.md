ceramic
=======

demand
------

Step 1
The ceramic demand equals today's production capacity at 8000 operating hours per year. The capacity is taken from JRC-IDEES (glass, paper), derived from the JRC-IDEES thermal energy consumption and the Rehfeldt et al. (2017) fuel intensity (ceramic), or from the FAOSTAT production (food). Nodes without JRC-IDEES data use a proxy node (CH as AT, NO as FI, UK as DE scaled by population); for paper, their consumption from the JRC BAT reference document is used.
