battery
=======

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of battery is based on Schmidt et al. (2019).

**Citation**

Oliver Schmidt, Sylvain Melchior, Adam Hawkes, Iain Staffell (2019). Projecting the Future Levelized Cost of Electricity Storage Technologies. Joule. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2018.12.008

capacity_existing
-----------------

Step 1
The existing power capacity of battery storage is the cumulative electrochemical storage capacity per country reported in the EMMES 9.5 report, split into capacity additions per construction year.

**Citation**

LCP Delta, Energy Storage Europe Association (2025). EMMES 9.5 - European Market Monitor on Energy Storage: Europe's energy storage hits 100 GW. Energy Storage Europe Association.. Note: The capacity values are read off the stacked bar chart 'Cumulative power capacity' (Figure 1) of the report.

efficiency_charge
-----------------

Step 1
The round-trip efficiency of battery is 0.86 in Schmidt et al. (2019) and is split evenly between charging and discharging.

**Citation**

Oliver Schmidt, Sylvain Melchior, Adam Hawkes, Iain Staffell (2019). Projecting the Future Levelized Cost of Electricity Storage Technologies. Joule. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2018.12.008

self_discharge
--------------

Step 1
The self-discharge rate of batteries is set to 2% per month for automotive applications, which corresponds to an hourly self-discharge rate of 2.806e-05.

**Citation**

Christoph Alt, Jürgen Janek (2026). Quantifying the self-discharge of solid-state batteries. Nature Energy. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1038/s41560-026-02038-1

capacity_existing_energy
------------------------

Step 1
The existing energy capacity of battery is calculated by multiplying the existing power capacity with the maximum energy-to-power ratio.
