oil_plant
=========

lifetime
--------

Step 1
Technical lifetime for 'oil_plant' is the median across all available data for the agencies diw, potencia reporting data for this technology at plant size 'None'. Monetary values are rebased to 2022 Euro using ECB HICP inflation.

**Citations**

-----------------

Step 1

conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The conversion factor is derived from the Eurostat dataset as the total consumption of oil divided by the total electricity generation from oil plants.

**Citation**

Eurostat (2026). Eurostat energy balances. Eurostat. https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_bal_c/default/table?lang=en
