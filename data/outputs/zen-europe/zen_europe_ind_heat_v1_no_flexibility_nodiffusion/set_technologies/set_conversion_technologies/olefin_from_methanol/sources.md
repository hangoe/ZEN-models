olefin_from_methanol
====================

lifetime
--------

Step 1
The lifetime of olefin from methanol is manually set to the lifetime of methanol from natural gas, assuming a standard industrial technology lifetime.

**Citation**

Guido Collodi, Giuliana Azzaro, Noemi Ferrari, Stanley Santos (2017). Demonstrating Large Scale Industrial CCS through CCU - A Case Study for Methanol Production. Energy Procedia. https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2021.115052

capex_specific_conversion
-------------------------

Step 1
The specific capital expenditure (capex) for olefin_from_methanol technologies is based on the work of Aidres et al. (2023).

**Citation**

Luc Girardin, Juan Correa Laguna, Joris Valee (2023). Advancing industrial decarbonisation by assessing the future use of renewable energies in industrial processes. Publications Office of the European Union. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/d80943a6-5116-11ee-9220-01aa75ed71a1/language-en

conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The conversion factor of olefin from methanol is obtained from the Aidres dataset (Table 32, MeOH-to-olefin route).

**Citation**

Luc Girardin, Juan Correa Laguna, Joris Valee (2023). Advancing industrial decarbonisation by assessing the future use of renewable energies in industrial processes. Publications Office of the European Union. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/d80943a6-5116-11ee-9220-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
