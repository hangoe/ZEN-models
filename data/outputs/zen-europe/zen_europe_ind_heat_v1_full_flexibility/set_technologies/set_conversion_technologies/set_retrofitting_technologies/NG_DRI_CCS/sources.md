NG_DRI_CCS
==========

lifetime
--------

Step 1
Technical lifetime for 'NG_DRI_CCS' is the median across all available data for the agencies dea reporting data for this technology at plant size 'None'. Monetary values are rebased to 2022 Euro using ECB HICP inflation.

capacity_addition_unbounded
---------------------------

Step 1
The unbounded capacity addition of carbon storage is based on the median existing and future capacity from the IOGP report, divided by the number of nodes in the dataset.

**Citation**

IOGP (2026). CO2 storage projects in Europe. IOGP. https://iogpeurope.org/carbon-capture-use-storage-feb2023/

max_diffusion_rate
------------------

Step 1
The maximum diffusion rate of NG_DRI_CCS is set to the historically observed diffusion rate of wind onshore from Mannhardt et al. (2024).

**Citation**

conversion_factor
-----------------

Step 1
The conversion factor of NG_DRI_CCS is derived from Agora Industry, 'Low-carbon technologies for the global steel transformation': an electricity demand of 1.45 GJ per ton of crude steel, divided by the 0.35 tCO2 captured per ton of crude steel to give the demand per captured carbon.

**Citation**

Wido K. WiteckaJulian SomersKathy ReimannNiklas WagnerOle ZeltAlexander JülichClemens SchneiderProf. Max Åhman (2024). Low-carbon technologies for the global steel transformation. Agora Industry. https://www.agora-industry.org/publications/low-carbon-technologies-for-the-global-steel-transformation

retrofit_flow_coupling_factor
-----------------------------

Step 1
The retrofit flow coupling factor of a steel CCS retrofit is the carbon capture rate of the retrofit, in tCO2 per ton of crude steel produced by the route it retrofits. This is used to scale the captured carbon output flow of the retrofit against the capacity units of its base technology.

**Citation**

Wido K. WiteckaJulian SomersKathy ReimannNiklas WagnerOle ZeltAlexander JülichClemens SchneiderProf. Max Åhman (2024). Low-carbon technologies for the global steel transformation. Agora Industry. https://www.agora-industry.org/publications/low-carbon-technologies-for-the-global-steel-transformation
