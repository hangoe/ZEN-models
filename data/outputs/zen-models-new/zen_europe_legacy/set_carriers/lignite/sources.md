lignite
=======

availability_import
-------------------

Step 1
The import availability of lignite is set to infinity, as the availability is not capped in the settings.

price_import
------------

Step 1
Import price of lignite from the TYNDP 2020 dataset, converted from €/GJ to €/MWh and adjusted for inflation.

**Citation**

ENTSO-E, ENTSOG (2020). Fuel Commodities and Carbon Prices. TYNDP 2020 Scenario Report. https://2020.entsos-tyndp-scenarios.eu/fuel-commodities-and-carbon-prices/

carbon_intensity_carrier_import
-------------------------------

Step 1
Carbon intensity of lignite from the IPCC emission factors dataset, converted from kgCO2/TJ to tonsCO2/MWh.

**Citation**

IPCC (2006). 2006 IPCC Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories - Volume 2: Energy. 2006 IPCC Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories. https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/. Note: Introduction, Table 1.4
