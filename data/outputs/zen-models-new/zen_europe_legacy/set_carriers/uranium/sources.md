uranium
=======

availability_import
-------------------

Step 1
The import availability of uranium is set to infinity, as the availability is not capped in the settings.

price_import
------------

Step 1
Import price of uranium from the TYNDP 2020 dataset, converted from €/GJ to €/MWh and adjusted for inflation.

**Citation**

ENTSO-E, ENTSOG (2020). Fuel Commodities and Carbon Prices. TYNDP 2020 Scenario Report. https://2020.entsos-tyndp-scenarios.eu/fuel-commodities-and-carbon-prices/
